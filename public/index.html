Meet our mascots.

Three friendly faces of AWSSBG-CDM.

Avie

Bubbly, cheerful and a little obsessed with pink, Avie brings warmth to our winter community. She loves making new friends, celebrating small wins and helping every builder feel welcome.

Nimbus

Curious and adventurous, Nimbus is always ready to try something new. With a fearless, live-in-the-moment spirit, he sees every challenge as the start of another adventure.

Sev

A sharp-minded, sometimes grumpy rule-follower, Sev thinks ahead and keeps the team grounded. Beneath his serious exterior, he is always willing to help fellow builders find a solution.
