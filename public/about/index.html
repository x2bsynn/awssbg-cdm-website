Our legacy

From a learning club to a community of builders.

Since 2024

Founded in 2024 by Darryl John Cainday, then Director for Relations of AWS Student User Group Philippines (formerly AWS Cloud Clubs Philippines), the initiative began as AWS Learning Club – Colegio de Muntinlupa. It was acknowledged within the national AWS student community before receiving formal college recognition.

In 2025, following AWS Student Community Day Mega Manila, leadership transitioned to Jan Christian Wagan, who had served as the event’s Lead Layout Artist. He spearheaded the club’s formalization through institutional coordination, documentation and the appointment of a faculty adviser. In November 2025, he became the first AWS-recognized Cloud Club Captain of CDM, marking the transition to AWS Cloud Club.

Formal recognition by Colegio de Muntinlupa followed during the second semester of AY 2025–2026. The organization was also recognized by the City Government of Muntinlupa as a school-based student organization. In April 2026, the global AWS rebrand expanded the program’s identity beyond cloud computing to include AI, full-stack development and other areas of technology.

Officially launched in August 2026 alongside AY 2026–2027, AWS Student Builder Group – Colegio de Muntinlupa continues the vision of its founders, Darryl John Cainday and Jan Christian Wagan: a student-led community built on learning, collaboration and innovation.

Mission & vision

Our guiding commitments to learning, innovation and service.

Mission

We empower students through inclusive AWS learning, hands-on workshops, networking and volunteerism, enabling them to apply their skills, thrive in the digital economy and create meaningful change in their communities.

Vision

As a student organization centered around Amazon Web Services (AWS) and cloud computing, we envision a long-lasting community of passionate learners from various campuses nationwide. These students will embrace innovation and leverage the power of AWS to drive technological advancements.

Our goals.

As a pioneering AWS student organization, we recognize the significance of establishing pragmatic goals to secure the successful realization of our vision and mission.

01

Education and Skill Development

Our primary goal is to facilitate student learning and skill development by offering workshops, seminars, and training sessions focused on AWS services, architecture, and best practices. We aim to empower students with the necessary knowledge to leverage AWS tools effectively and build innovative solutions.

02

Community Building Across the Country

We aim to build a vibrant and supportive community of students interested in AWS. Through regular meetings, events, and online forums, we foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and networking opportunities. Our goal is to create a space where students can connect, exchange ideas, and form meaningful relationships and networks.

03

Industry Engagement

We strive to establish strong connections with industry professionals, AWS experts, and organizations leveraging AWS. By organizing guest lectures, industry visits, and internships, we aim to bridge the gap between academia and industry, providing students with valuable insights and potential career opportunities.

04

Innovation and Projects

We encourage students to undertake innovative projects and leverage AWS services to solve real-world challenges. Our goal is to foster an environment where students can experiment, develop prototypes, and display their ideas using AWS technology.

05

Student-Centric

Our organization’s student-centric goal is to prioritize the needs, growth, and success of students. We aim to create a supportive and inclusive environment where students can thrive academically, professionally, and personally. Through tailored programs, resources, and opportunities, we strive to empower students to reach their full potential, develop essential skills, and pursue their passions. Our organization is committed to fostering a student-focused approach that enhances learning, promotes collaboration, and prepares students for a successful future.
